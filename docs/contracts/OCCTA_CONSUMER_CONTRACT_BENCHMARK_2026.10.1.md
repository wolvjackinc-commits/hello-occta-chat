# OCCTA Consumer Contract Benchmark — Production v2026.10.1

**Effective for new residential consumer contracts from 6 October 2026.**

Historic accepted Contract Summaries, Contract Information Packs, PDFs, hashes and acceptance evidence remain immutable and retain the version originally accepted.

## Locked product/contract rules
- Flex 30: 30-day rolling, no fixed minimum term, normal 30-day notice, no remaining-month ETF; One Touch Switch rules apply where relevant.
- Price Lock 24: 24-month minimum term from go-live, then 30-day rolling unless the customer expressly agrees a new fixed term.
- No scheduled CPI/RPI/inflation-linked/percentage-based in-contract price rise.
- Fixed-term ETF: fair-loss calculation using remaining recurring broadband charges less VAT no longer due and direct costs reasonably avoided; never above remaining recurring charges; no double recovery.
- Network cease/migration: separate from ETF; only where lawful and actually incurred; actual qualifying cost capped at £114 inc VAT within 12 months and £60 inc VAT thereafter.
- Billing starts only after confirmed go-live. Direct Debit is active only after provider confirmation.
- Moving home: £0 home-move migration fee if no new line/ONT/network work/equipment is required; chargeable new work is disclosed. A materially worse/different service is not treated as equivalent without consent.
- Engineer/no-access/customer-caused charges: quoted where known; otherwise actual qualifying third-party/network cost if fair, lawful and disclosed. No separate OCCTA engineer admin fee unless disclosed before acceptance.
- Digital Voice depends on broadband/equipment/mains power; 999/112 are free when operational; vulnerability/resilience assessed case-by-case.
- Cooling-off rights preserved; early supply/installation requires an express request where available.
- Add-ons/equipment do not silently extend the minimum term.
- ADR wording stays short and generic until verified scheme details are centrally configured.

## Document architecture
1. Contract Summary.
2. Contract Information & Customer Agreement Pack.
3. Service schedules/price guides where applicable.
4. Cooling-off/model cancellation form.
5. Acceptance Certificate.
6. Immutable internal evidence manifest.

## Acceptance
The customer must have access to both pre-contract documents before acceptance. The final electronic order action is **ORDER WITH OBLIGATION TO PAY**. Existing OTP and evidence controls remain. Accepted PDFs/hashes remain immutable.

## Historical records
Never overwrite, regenerate, backdate or retrospectively strengthen accepted evidence. Old unaccepted document versions may be superseded so the current production wording is issued before acceptance.
